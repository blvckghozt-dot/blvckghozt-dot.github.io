Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Math a Day is an educational video channel. The "Math a Day" application is an automated publishing tool operated solely by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos.

What data the app uses

The app uses the YouTube API Services and the Facebook Graph API only to upload videos, titles, descriptions and tags to the owner's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page. Authorization tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are used for nothing else.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about viewers or any other users. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising identifiers.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Please also see the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a message to the Math a Day Facebook Page.
